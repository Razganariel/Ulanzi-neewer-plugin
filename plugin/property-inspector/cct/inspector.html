Dial

The wire carries the temperature in one byte of 100 K, so 100 K is the finest step.

Press the dial to walk the scene list. Use CCT Up/Down for single steps or CCT Presets for the same list on a key.

Presets

Fill a row and press Save. Nothing is written until you do. Delete removes a scene at once. These are the scenes a dial press walks; the CCT Presets key keeps its own list.

# Name Temp (K) Lum (%)

Each scene carries its own brightness, because a scene read with a colour meter is not just a temperature: a candle sits around 28% while a cold blue needs the full 100%. Leave the brightness empty to keep whatever the dial last set.

A scene outside the minimum and maximum set above is pulled to the nearer bound when the press applies it, so a scene can be listed without ever appearing. Keep the range wide enough for the list.
